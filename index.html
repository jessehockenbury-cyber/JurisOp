Frequently Asked Questions

01. I am an early-stage founder. Is JurisOps a fit for where I am right now?

Yes. We specifically partner with pre-seed, bootstrapped, and scaling founders to build an institutional-grade foundation early on. Rather than having to untangle messy equity splits, informal founder handshakes, or missing IP assignments later during due diligence, we ensure your entity and governing agreements are drafted properly from the start with accessible, practical scoping.

02. Can JurisOps draft legal documents and provide legal advice?

Yes. Unlike automated form-generation platforms, JurisOps provides strategic counsel and bespoke legal drafting—including customized operating agreements, bylaws, founder equity vesting, IP assignment agreements, and commercial contracts under a tailored engagement agreement. We do not provide courtroom litigation, allowing us to stay focused on transactional strategy, risk mitigation, and commercial execution.

03. Why choose JurisOps over DIY automated filers like Stripe Atlas or LegalZoom?

Automated platforms file basic paperwork and hand you generic boilerplate forms that often fail when co-founder disputes or investor diligence arise. JurisOps offers strategic, customized counsel: we guide you on entity type and jurisdiction (KY, OH, DE), draft tailored governance terms that reflect your true business deal, protect your intellectual property, and configure your banking and compliance infrastructure.

04. How does your pricing work?

We believe in accessible, predictable, and transparent pricing without the surprises of open-ended billable hours. Depending on your needs, we offer cost-effective project scopes, phased formation packages, or flexible advisory retainers designed to align with early-stage budgets.

05. Do I need to be located in Ohio or Kentucky?

While our firm is based in the Greater Cincinnati and Northern Kentucky corridor, our startup structuring, operational playbooks, and systems implementations support founders and corporate clients across the United States.
