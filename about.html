Executive Leadership

Translating legal boundaries into operational velocity.

Jesse Hockenbury brings 15 years of combined experience across law practice, enterprise IT project management, and business operations to the leadership of JurisOps Consulting.

Throughout a multidisciplinary career, spanning legal practice as an attorney at the national law firm BakerHostetler, leadership as an IT Project Manager, and teaching as a Computer Science Lecturer, Jesse recognized a persistent challenge: founders and growing enterprises frequently struggle with the disconnect between legal requirements and day-to-day business operations.

Traditional legal models often deliver isolated work product billed in rigid increments, uncoupled from practical execution or commercial software systems. Meanwhile, generic automated document engines generate one-size-fits-all paperwork that easily falters when co-founder relations, investor diligence, or commercial scale are tested.

Jesse founded JurisOps Consulting to deliver strategic counsel integrated with business realities. He works directly with early-stage founders to structure sound entity foundations, tailor governance, and protect core assets, while helping expanding organizations streamline workflows, manage operational risk, and deploy modern systems that scale smoothly.
